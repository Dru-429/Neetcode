How this works

Your camera tracks your hand positions locally in the browser using on-device hand tracking. Nothing is streamed or saved anywhere.

  1. Click Enable Camera and allow access.
  2. Bring both hands up and form the sign shown in the reference panel — fingertips touching at the top, wrists together below.
  3. Hold the shape steady. The ring around the kanji fills as you hold it.
  4. Once full, the domain opens behind you. Click Close Domain to reset.

If detection feels too strict or too loose, adjust it from Settings.
